Lantern Vigil – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22/10/2026

Lantern Vigil ("the game") is developed by zak1aN ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information I do not collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. I do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and I do not run my own servers that receive your data.

Your game progress

Your progress (essence, atlas, unlocked skills, settings and similar) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to me. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional reward ads that you choose to watch. AdMob is provided by Google and may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings ("Ad privacy choices"). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. I do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play shares with the game only what is needed to deliver your purchase (for example, the product and an order identifier). See the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
